## 3. Fixed calibrators and endpoints

Feature acquisition is an explicit shared **eight-sequence Le-WM feature pass after the shortlist is fixed**, including the otherwise unscored returned mean. It never feeds back into CEM, changes the bank or requests extra samples. Direct-goal and inverse-residual features are computed together from that pass and charged separately. Vanilla reference tails do not pay for an unused checker. Store five-primitive-spaced observed baseline frames on fitting sources for the proposed IDM fit (at most31 RGB224 frames/source, about4.67MB uncompressed), within the10MB/source cap; do not save every image on every branch. These reservations are additional to action/endpoint evidence, not an assumption that hashes can reconstruct images.

Simultaneous: bank maximum advantage residual, alpha=.05, one initial bank per calibration source. LTT: conditional harmful-override target .05, familywise delta=.05, five thresholds {0,.05,.10,.20,.40}; choose smallest accepted, otherwise baseline. PC: native binary-success utility, alpha=.05; learn policy on96 development sources, exact finite-label calibration on320; preserve learned-policy ties. No smoothing of utility to escape degeneracy.

With zero observed harms, five-rule LTT needs at least90 **overrides**, not90 candidate rows; one rule needs59. At2% override frequency, about4,500 independent calibration sources would be needed in expectation for90 overrides, exceeding the available pool. With320 sources there may be no informative conditional-risk certificate: report that, do not relax alpha or pool roles. A finite95% simultaneous rank needs19 banks but does not ensure positive bounds. Uniform logging gives40 expected policy matches from320 sources; neither40 nor19 is guaranteed. Binary PC can return only zero certificates even with many samples.

Single-decision evaluation is fixed-tail native success on all160 final-development sources. Report each source, selected success, sampled gain/loss, override and conditional risk; no seed/branch pseudoreplication. Primary comparisons: LTT versus ordinary point, PC-complete versus ordinary point; baseline differences descriptive with simultaneous intervals. No differentiated proposed treatment exists yet.

The **separate repeated-policy stage does not advance automatically**. It requires an explicit direction after preserved single-decision results, regardless of AUROC, coverage or apparent gains. If directed, evaluate on those same160 development sources, each policy on its own actual states: vanilla, native ACID, point, simultaneous, LTT, PC-complete, point-logged, PC-logged. Eight policies ×160 =1,280 fresh full episodes, fixed seeds/caps; no claim of untouched confirmation. Native ACID versus vanilla is its primary comparison; all other full-policy contrasts are secondary and multiplicity-accounted. A second review cannot silently reuse the stage as a fresh confirmatory test.
