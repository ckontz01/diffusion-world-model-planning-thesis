Canonical reconciliation: RB2 `DATA-ROLES.json` enumerates 13 prior role lists (overlaps intentional), whose union is **320**. It includes CVL train/validation/reserved-closed-loop, BP1 old/new fit/evaluation and inherited reservations, SI1's 192, and LGP/RB1 development/technical IDs. RB2's 512 are disjoint, yielding **832 excluded and 768 remaining**. BP1 allocation and SI1 FOLDS independently cross-check their entries in `inventory_crosscheck.py`. Do not sum overlapping role counts.

`DATA-ROLES-PROPOSED.json` contains every exact excluded and proposed ID, metadata digest, and deterministic allocation rule. Sort the remaining IDs by SHA256(`av0-source-role-v1|<id>`), integer tie; assign consecutive **192 fit / 96 policy-development / 320 calibration / 160 final-development-evaluation**. All anchors, branches, seeds, preprocessing and repeated draws for one source remain together. RB2's 512 are never recycled. All IDs1600–5999 and reserved roles stay unopened/unallocated. This is source-disjoint **development**, not untouched confirmation.

No old action-selection probability can be invented for deterministic top-k CEM choices. The future logged channel must use a fresh preassigned uniform random slot among the eight bank slots, independent of outcomes. The complete-branch adaptation instead uses one fixed-policy branch per calibration source and no logging weight. Whole-source labels and action associations, not trajectory counts, determine the effective sample size.

The five supplied supporting files match the supplied SHA-256 manifest (recorded in DATA-ROLES-PROPOSED). They remain guidance; their original files were not changed or executed.
