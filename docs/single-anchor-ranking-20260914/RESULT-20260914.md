# Single-anchor ranking experiment — completed development result

## Decision and primary result

The approved experiment is complete and technically valid. **The results do not
support replacing continuation selection with immediate selection.** Immediate
selection improved first-chunk physical progress, but the average benefit did
not survive the unchanged downstream continuation policy.

This was a fixed, outcome-informed development intervention, not confirmation:
32 exposed references, H75/H150 equally weighted, historical anchors 0/30,
checkpoint 7201, two arms and two fresh-process repeats. There are 128 paired
intervention points, 256 logical arm branches and 512 executions including the
repeatability run. Only repeat 0 contributes scientific estimates; n=32 reference
clusters, not 128 independent anchors or 512 independent episodes.

| Primary native success within full remaining budget | Continuation selection | Immediate selection |
|---|---:|---:|
| Both horizons/anchors | 16/128 (12.500%) | 12/128 (9.375%) |
| H75 | 8/64 (12.500%) | 10/64 (15.625%) |
| H150 | 8/64 (12.500%) | 2/64 (3.125%) |

Immediate minus continuation: **−3.125 percentage points**, exploratory
reference-cluster bootstrap 95% interval **[−10.9375, +4.6875] points**.
The frozen procedure uses 10,000 reference resamples with seed 20260914.
At the 128 paired points there were **7 wins, 11 losses, 110 ties**.
After averaging anchors within each horizon and equally weighting horizons,
there were **5 positive, 6 negative and 21 zero reference effects**.
The interval includes zero: this is not proof of general inferiority, equivalence
or an all-task claim. The unfavorable point estimate is not a technical failure
to repair. H75 and H150 contrasts were +3.125 and −9.375 points respectively;
they are descriptive horizon splits, not separately selected primary claims.

The exact historical predicate was retained: combined four-position-coordinate
error norm <20 and circular angle error <pi/9 at the same post-action step.
Success during the first chunk is included. Initial anchor states are excluded.
No survivor-only restriction or relaxed endpoint replaced the primary result.

## First chunk, hand-off and full remainder are different endpoints

Physical joint margin is max(position error /20, circular angle error /(pi/9));
lower is better. Improvement below means continuation minus immediate margin.

| Repeat-0 measure | Continuation | Immediate |
|---|---:|---:|
| Success during first chunk | 1/128 | 2/128 |
| Mean closest joint margin during first chunk | 9.064462 | 8.499305 |
| First-chunk terminal branches | 1 | 2 |
| Branches still active at replanning hand-off | 127 | 126 |
| Mean hand-off joint margin among each arm's active branches | 9.925553 | 9.246042 |
| Mean closest joint margin over full remaining execution | 4.384943 | 4.484424 |

First-chunk margin improved by **0.565157**, with positive reference-level
improvement on **29/32 references**. Horizon-specific improvements were +0.813682
(H75) and +0.316631 (H150). This reproduces the direction of the exposed local
progress signal without establishing a full-episode benefit.

Among the **126 pairs with both arms active at hand-off**, the paired hand-off
margin improvement was +0.738886. This is explicitly a descriptive survivor-pair
subset; the two marginal means above use different active sets. Neither is
substituted for the full-sample primary endpoint.

Full-remainder margin improvement was **−0.099481** (17 positive/15 negative
references), exploratory cluster interval **[−0.739120, +0.490274]**.
It was +0.525380 on H75 and −0.724342 on H150. Thus even the average full-remainder
continuous metric did not retain the favorable immediate-progress direction.
There is no causal identification here of which later dynamics or prediction
errors explain the reversal.

unchanged. No tolerance was tuned after seeing results. The t30 comparisons
freshly replayed the historical continuation prefix, never the t0 treatment.
Both arms computed both anchor ranks from the original matched workload, selected
only the first chunk, and then used the original continuation policy. No imagined
second chunk was executed as a fixed tail.

## Interpretation for the next research decision

The local physical-progress signal is repeatable on this fixed development set,
but **it is insufficient as a replacement selection objective**: better early
margin coexists with lower aggregate native success and no average full-remainder
margin improvement, particularly adverse at H150. Keep the primary endpoint
central. This experiment supports investigating how local progress interacts
with later continuation choices; it does not identify a uniquely justified model
or planner correction, authorize a redesign, or resolve the historical SAGE gap.
