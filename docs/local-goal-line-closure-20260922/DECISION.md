# Local-goal proposer line: closed for now

22 September 2026. This is a prospective research-priority decision covering
LGP1, LGP-RB1 and LGP-RB2, not a revision of their historical verdicts.

The five-population diffusion lead did not replicate on the 512-source
development cohort. RB2's primary diffusion-minus-GMM difference was −0.7487
percentage points, with the original 95% source-bootstrap interval
[−2.0833, +0.5859]. The family-by-budget interaction hypothesis was not
supported: +0.0326 points [−1.9531, +2.0508]. Neither finding establishes
equivalence, universal inferiority, or the absence of any interaction.

Neither local proposer is promoted. Continuation remains the working system,
but continuation was not a live RB2 arm. Matched local GMM is not native SAGE.

This proposer-and-refinement-budget development line is closed for now.

return-rule, sampler or training search is authorized by this decision.
