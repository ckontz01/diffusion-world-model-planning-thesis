Empirical hypothesis only: a compact learned prefix-response model may improve continuation choices beyond ordinary history conditioning and established Bayesian feedback around the same frozen planner. A residual is a transformation of an actual observation, not privileged new evidence. A changed tree is changed search packaging. The decision-valued acquisition is known, and the proposed model is not entitled to outperform a correctly specified exact Bayesian control.

The artificial neural results do not establish a differentiated treatment: the informative case ties ordinary conditioning and Bayesian controls, the unstable case regresses, and the unannounced reversal fails. No “first,” universal guarantee, action-safety claim, robotics mechanism or paper-superiority claim is supported.
