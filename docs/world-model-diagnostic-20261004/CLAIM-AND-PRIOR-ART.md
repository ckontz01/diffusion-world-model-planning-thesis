# Claim and closest prior art

WM-DIAG0 asks how much full-task decision performance is **recoverable by this specified consequence/scoring intervention within one finite fixed bank**. It does not identify a unique root cause, apportion failure percentages, or test a deployable oracle.

The consequence replacement and alternative-objective ideas are replication/control work. The specific joint four-cell, fixed-action, full-budget-tail control isolates operational changes more carefully; its combination is not asserted to be first or novel. A differentiated later result would require prospectively predicting a useful feasible repair on genuinely excluded settings, including equal-budget repair testing. No conference result is promised.

Primary versions and method-level findings below were inspected on 2026-10-04, alongside the existing ACV/CVL literature ledger. “Unverified” does not mean absent.

## Historical evidence boundaries

E16 is local-target ranking, not eventual native-success opportunity. September proposer-context replacement is not predictor replacement inside a score. ACV/CVL labels are specific to their action chunk and tail, not transferable outcome labels. DTV saved-input timing is not closed-loop compute. Historical success rates are not pooled and no unexecuted candidate receives an old outcome.

## Follow-on only

Develop a diagnostic-to-repair rule on discovery settings; freeze a small menu of feasible non-oracle repairs; record recommendations before observing their repair outcomes; test on excluded tasks/backbones and independent parents. Compare against the best development-selected fixed repair and directly testing the same repairs under the **same total diagnostic budget**, including neural calls, data, labels and physical experiments. A task-name lookup is not the intended capability. This package neither trains that rule nor gathers every repair outcome.
