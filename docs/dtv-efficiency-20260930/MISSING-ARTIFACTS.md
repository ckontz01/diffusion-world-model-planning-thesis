# Missing measurements and remaining uncertainties

## Recovered, not missing

All nine true epsilon-checker checkpoints and all 18 required ACID/forward
controls exist at authenticated original locations. Their training summaries,
checkpoint byte hashes, architectures, parameters and seed associations are
bound. Three original Le-WM checkpoint bytes were authenticated. The D1
capture/score and v3 shared-score artifacts, six exact input manifests, literal
scoring source, original CEM/policy source and all nine single-noise execution
summaries were recovered or whole-byte authenticated without deserialization.
E6D evaluator elapsed records and reused E6 anchors exist; none was guessed.

## Unavailable or incompatible historical evidence

1. Dedicated D1 sigma-.25 checker/complete-call/CEM latency and dedicated
   single-environment episode latency were not found. Its recorded
   24-environment evaluation elapsed total is incompatible with those scopes.
2. v3 Stage B never executed. Its legacy-DTV lambda-.005 complete planning
   latency is therefore not an existing measured result.
3. E3/E6D have evaluator totals, not a separately timed epsilon-DTV checker,
   solver, setup, energy or per-component decomposition. These are boundaries,
   not substitute DTV measurements; they are excluded from the proposed grid.
4. An official ACID implementation has not been obtained or authenticated.
   The historical reconstruction is disclosed and remains the comparator.
5. The old published rounded numbers and raw summaries are distinct evidence.
   Raw timings take precedence; no historical claims were retrospectively edited.

## Future included technical checks — not silently resolved now

- Saved-input schemas and image/history fields are supported by source and
  metadata, but tensor content is unopened. The real worker must check exact
  rows, shapes, native pixels/goal interface and reproduction of saved B0
  costs/trajectories. Missing or cached-latent-only inputs stop the job; no
  tensor regeneration or boundary substitution is permitted.
- Float32 stored state is supported by executed training source, not verified
  by deserializing here. Future loading checks precision, architecture and
  metadata without silently converting an incompatible checkpoint.
- Byte inventories pin installed code. Future live package/dependency/device
  identity, numerical equality, CUDA determinism, RAM/VRAM and performance
  remain untested. Artificial CPU tests are not a CUDA/inference reproduction.
- Shared-world setup, allocator warm state and thermal/scheduler variation
  may differ from 2026 historical records. Balanced warm blocks, separate first
  operational calls, event/wall boundaries and device properties record them;
  they do not establish a future runtime forecast now.
- Nine 800-second reservations fit the two-hour allocation ceiling exactly.
  Historical medians suggest room but do not prove all real equivalence/setup
  work fits. A timeout/fault is retained and charged; no repetitions are
  dropped, no fastest seed is selected and no cap is expanded automatically.
- Native result transfer availability and whole/member verification must be
  checked after an authorized timing run. This preparation verifies only its
  new small package. No old archive is retransferred as a convenience.

A favorable offline ratio does not resolve these gaps or authorize physics.
Any future success–cost study must receive a separate bounded instruction and
include sensible lower-budget plain/forward/ACID points. No sweep is designed,
training replacement is fitted or model is promoted by this preparation.
