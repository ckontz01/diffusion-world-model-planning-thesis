## Estimator, contrasts, sensitivity and multiplicity

For source i and pair s, let S,C,N,A,O be binary full-budget native success; E_i is the one early-replan outcome. First form paired differences **within the same source and seed pair**, then average three seed-pair differences within source, then average512 sources. For early comparison use (Σ_s A_is)/3−E_i; E_i is referenced three times algebraically but observed and counted once. Output all512×3×5 outcomes plus512 early outcomes, exact identities, choices/guards and source-by-seed effects.

The two primary mechanism contrasts are A−N (responding after active prefix selection) and A−C (anticipated-feedback versus committed prefix with response updates retained). Prespecified supporting contrasts are C−S, N−S and (A−N)−(C−S). External comparisons are A−O and A−E. One fixed family of **seven**; no endpoint/seed/contrast selection.

Report each fixed pair's mean and source effects, the equal-three-pair mean, seed mean range and sample SD. Three deliberately fixed fits do not establish population training-seed robustness; no seed-bootstrap CI or best-seed claim. Whole-source bootstrap10,000 draws/seed94431 preserves every seed and arm within each resampled source. Report nominal95% and Bonferroni99.285714% percentile intervals, labeled nominal rather than guaranteed. They cannot invent unseen losses.

For a conservative familywise benchmark, also report simultaneous95% Hoeffding intervals for all seven source-averaged contrasts: halfwidth `range*sqrt(log(2*7/.05)/(2*512))`, clipped to the contrast range. Range=2 for binary differences and4 for the interaction. Independence is between source units, conditional on these fixed trained models; it is not between seeds or branch rows. The bounding principle is from [Hoeffding's original paper](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/01621459.1963.10500830); the union bound over the14 tails gives the stated family construction. For this deterministic development cohort the finite-cohort mean is exact descriptive; population coverage needs the stated independent-source sampling model and must not be advertised as untouched confirmation or individual-action safety.

No formal positive mechanism conclusion from a merely favorable nominal interval. A conservative positive mean-mechanism flag requires both primary familywise lower bounds above0 and positive point estimates for each of the three fixed pairs; still no promotion or safety claim. This flag does not control a separate population-over-training-seeds hypothesis. Early replanning must be reported even when it wins or remains unresolved.

Fixed practical effect size of interest:5 percentage points (descriptive, not a post-hoc launch threshold). `SENSITIVITY.json` gives a paired-normal80%-power approximation at family7 correction over discordances .05,.10,.25,.50,1, without assuming extra independent data from model seeds. Values are planning scales, not guaranteed power or estimates from old32. Conservative Hoeffding halfwidth is about14.8pp (interaction29.7pp), so512 sources cannot certify small gains with that worst-case bound. The study can remain inconclusive; it will not expand N or add fits to chase significance.
