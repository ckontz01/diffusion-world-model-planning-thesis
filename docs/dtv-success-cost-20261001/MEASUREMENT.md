# Operational boundaries and evidence

Each get-action interval is synchronized before and after. It includes current observation image processing/normalization/resizing, any CEM solve, original buffered-policy processing and the historical float32 action decoder. This happens on buffered steps too; it is not cached-latent timing. Cumulative planning time sums **all** 50-or-fewer get-action intervals. Planning decisions are steps 0 and 25 when reached. Preserve their separate solve-only synchronized wall, decision observation-to-action latency, normalized final-elite plan, scored population/candidate counts and first/later classifications.

Operational episode time = reset through valid initial observation + all get-action intervals + all environment-step intervals, including physics. Environment construction and shared model/input loading are separately recorded. Also retain elapsed episode time including audit/journal interruptions; it is not relabeled pure operational latency. The operational boundary intentionally excludes independent endpoint reconstruction, evidence conversion, journal writing and hashing; those audit seconds are reported. Existing accepted scorer diagnostic collection and solver logging remain inside their callable, not optimized away.

Source/checkpoint/runtime authentication and setup are outside episode hot intervals but remain charged allocation work. Input fingerprinting and final serialization/sealing are outside the headline region; all raw timing and action-intent/transition journals are preserved, including interrupted tails. Solver action tensor conversion for audit occurs after the timed get-action interval. Evidence requirements do not differ by treatment.

Store native endpoint evidence at reset and after every delivered action; independent acceptance reconstructs success, termination reason, cadence, budgets, float32 decoded delivered plans, raw journal/timing correspondence and complete identities without using learned rankings. This is trace reconstruction, not a second simulator execution or a claim of independently re-simulated dynamics. Cubes' target quaternion is saved but native positional success alone is tested. Initial success cases remain in all source denominators with zero plans.

Scoped process CPU and Linux RSS high-water are measured, as are Torch allocator peak allocated/reserved bytes per episode. RSS high-water can include earlier job work; Torch reserved memory includes cache and shared loaded models. These are not whole-node CPU/memory, total device memory use, energy or power. No such broader claim is made.

Every episode keeps delivered actions, successful and unsuccessful outcomes, legitimate stop flags, decision/candidate counts, costs and resources. Different termination times can change episode work. Lower episode time alone does not show algorithmic acceleration. First-decision comparisons start from common physical states; later inputs legitimately differ and are described as visited-state operational costs.
