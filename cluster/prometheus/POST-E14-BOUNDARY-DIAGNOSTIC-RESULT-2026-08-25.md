# Post-E14 VAD boundary diagnostic result

Date: 25 August 2026  
Role: P1 development-artifact diagnosis only  
Status: complete; supports designing one separately frozen E15  
Claim status: no paper-performance claim; E14 remains stopped; D5 remains sealed

## Executive result

The frozen E14 Cube failure was not merely an arbitrary robust-quantile
artifact. Raw VAD proposals exceeded Cube's declared `Box(-1,1)` action domain
often enough that the worst query-specific banks crossed E14's registered 25%
ceiling. However, being *at* a Cube limit is not itself pathological: the
expert data are legal and place substantial mass exactly at several limits.
The problem to address is raw overshoot followed by hard clipping, not expert-
like saturation.

The failure was generated by the VAD proposal distribution, not primarily by
Le-WM selecting unusually extreme candidates. Mean selected-candidate and
full-bank boundary rates were almost identical. All raw and clipped banks
retained 300/300 rounded-unique candidates, and hard clipping reduced mean
candidate variance by only about 1.42% on Cube and 0.82% on PushT.

This diagnosis supports the already limited next step: one new E15 in which
VAD, the matched diagonal Gaussian, and a direct-goal eight-mode GMM all use
the same smooth legal-action transform. It does not revise E14, authorize D5,
or establish that the redesign will work.

## Raw proposal overshoot

The primary legal-OOB statistic uses the declared environment bounds mapped
through the exact released float32 `StandardScaler`, with the repository's
four-float32-epsilon raw-action tolerance. Strict and tolerant results were
nearly identical.

| Task | Bank legal OOB mean | Bank row q99 | Bank row maximum by seed | Selected legal OOB mean | Selected row maximum by seed |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| PushT | 0.0544% | 2.50% | 9.67%, 9.53%, 8.47% | 0.0557% | 10.00%, 10.00%, 10.00% |
| Cube | 2.4928% | 15.52%--15.93% | 27.17%, 36.38%, 35.47% | 2.5048% | 38.67%, 35.00%, 48.00% |

Cube's mean selected-minus-bank robust-boundary difference was only
`+0.0123` percentage points; PushT's was `-0.0003` points. Thus Le-WM did not
materially create the aggregate boundary mass by preferring extreme options.

Cube legal overshoot was coordinate-specific. Across the three seeds, the
full-bank per-coordinate rates were approximately:

| Cube action coordinate | Legal OOB range across seeds |
|---:|---:|
| 0 | 0.233%--0.244% |
| 1 | 2.520%--2.592% |
| 2 | 6.774%--6.900% |
| 3 | 2.811%--2.960% |
| 4 | 0.000% |

After E14's hard robust clipping, Cube seed 6101 had 3.55% of bank elements
within 1% of a legal limit and 6.75% within 5%. Its mean legal overshoot before
clipping was 2.50%. PushT's robust bounds were well inside its declared legal
range, so its executed hard-clipped candidates had zero mass within 5% of the
declared limits despite the small raw overshoot tail.

## Expert-action context

The exact released float32 transform removed the false one-ULP violations
seen in an excluded intermediate audit.

- Cube P1 expert actions had zero strict and tolerant legal-OOB rate in all
  five coordinates.
- Cube coordinates 1, 2, and 3 had their 1st and/or 99th percentiles exactly
  on declared limits. Therefore substantial expert saturation is real and
  should not be optimized away merely to lower a boundary statistic.
- PushT's released P1 expert data contain a small genuine tail outside the
  declared `Box(-1,1)`: 0.0842% on coordinate 0 and 0.0726% on coordinate 1.
  The deployed PushT `step` method records and uses the action without clipping.
  E15 must report how often its common transform projects these rows; it may
  not silently call every expert tail value invalid.

## Interpretation for E14

E14's frozen result does not change. Its rule used the maximum per-bank robust-
boundary fraction, and VAD exceeded 25% on Cube for all three seeds. Gate C
therefore remains prohibited under E14, even though the global mean rate was
much smaller and VAD otherwise beat its matched Gaussian and conditioning
nulls.

The diagnostic sharpens the meaning of that stop:

1. Cube expert saturation is legitimate.
2. VAD nevertheless produced genuine raw legal overshoot, particularly on
   coordinate 2 and on a minority of difficult query banks.
3. Hard clipping created the registered exact-boundary statistic.
4. Le-WM selection was not the main cause.
5. A smooth bounded parameterization is a defensible new method, while simply
   weakening E14's threshold is not.

## Consequence for the next study

E15 may be drafted and frozen separately with all of the following fixed
before training:

- one boundary-aware variable-duration velocity-diffusion proposer;
- the same transform for a matched diagonal Gaussian and a direct-goal,
  trajectory-level eight-mode GMM;
- explicit pre-squash, exact-boundary, near-boundary, Jacobian-saturation,
  projection-rate, and diversity diagnostics relative to expert behavior;
- a fresh episode-disjoint offline development split;
- fixed P2 horizons 25, 75, and 150 only after the offline gate passes;
- released Base CEM, full published-equation SAGE reconstruction, a SAGE
  one-stage ablation, VAD, direct-goal GMM, and diagonal Gaussian;
- no D5 creation or consumption. Passing development may only authorize a
  separately frozen confirmation protocol.

The one-redesign stop rule remains binding. If boundary-aware VAD fails the
fresh offline gate, the long-horizon method line closes rather than being tuned
again.
